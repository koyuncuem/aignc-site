Research

Four research lines, one autonomy stack

Every topic below is an active thread in the group — each carried by graduate researchers from formulation to flight-ready implementation.

Line 01

Guidance & Flight Control

Optimization-based and learning-based control for agile flight — keeping high-performance vehicles safe at the edge of their envelope.

Model predictive controlAgile guidance algorithms Optimal trajectory generationFlight envelope protection Auto-GCASTerrain following control Impact time/angle controlIntegral RL control System-ID-based control
Line 02

Intelligent Navigation

Navigation that survives losing GPS — visual, inertial, terrain-referenced, and collaborative approaches for single vehicles and teams.

Visual-inertial navigationVisual-guided control Terrain-referenced navigationCollaborative navigation GPS-denied operationsVisual pursuit guidance
Line 03

Airspace Autonomy — ATM / UTM

High-level autonomy for the airspace itself: from AI-driven traffic network models to automated U-space services for uncrewed aviation.

AI-driven air traffic network modelsAirspace complexity modelling Dynamic sectorizationAutonomous flight-plan approval Strategic deconfliction4D trajectory management Network resilienceTurnaround visual intelligence
Line 04

Mission Autonomy & AI Teaming

Multi-agent decision-making where machines cooperate, compete, and share control authority with humans — in simulation and on real platforms.

RL-based air combat simulationAir combat modelling Control authority sharingCollaborative / competitive agents Human–AI teamingSwarm autonomy Data-driven predictive maintenance

Projects

Research that reaches the field

SESAR 3 JUActive

AI for U-space Services

European consortium research on AI-enabled U-space: automated drone flight-plan approval, risk assessment, and strategic deconfliction for dense urban airspace.

Erasmus+ CoVEActive

AIRMOB — Advanced Air Mobility

Türkiye lead of the AIRMOB Centre of Vocational Excellence, building the skills and training ecosystem for advanced air mobility across Europe and Türkiye.

IndustryActive

Autonomy with Turkish Aerospace

Collaborative autonomy and GNC research with Turkish Aerospace — recognized with ITU's 2025 University–Industry Collaboration Award.

ASELSANCompleted

Bal-UAV — Shape-shifting UAVs

Ballistically launchable UAVs that change their physical configuration in flight — developed and flight-demonstrated at ITU ARC, with a launch system firing up to five UAVs.

ASELSANCompleted

GPS-independent Anti-UAV Drone

AI-based visual target tracking with visual-inertial navigation for defending critical facilities where jamming renders GPS unusable — delivered after flight tests at Örencik.

Airlines & AirportsCompleted

Data-driven Flight Operations

Trajectory, delay, and network analytics with Turkish Airlines and iGA Istanbul Airport — large-scale flight data applied to efficiency and resilience.

Labs & Facilities

From simulation to the sky

Faculty Building · 312

AIGNC Lab

The group's home base for guidance, navigation and control development — algorithm design, hardware-in-the-loop testing, and avionics integration.

ARC Building · 101

Cyber Airspace Lab

Large-scale airspace simulation: air traffic network models, U-space services, and human-in-the-loop experiments for future ATM concepts.

Örencik

UAV Test Site

The group's outdoor proving ground — from agile quadrotors to shape-shifting UAV demonstrators, algorithms close the loop on real vehicles here.
