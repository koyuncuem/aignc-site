EK

Prof. Dr. Emre Koyuncu

Principal Investigator · Director, ITU Aerospace Research Center

Professor of Aeronautical Engineering at ITU and CTO of CORVUS Advanced Technologies. Formerly a visiting researcher at Boeing Research & Technology Europe and MIT AeroAstro; Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering (ITU, 2015) as a SESAR JU HALA! fellow. His research bridges optimization-based control, reinforcement learning, and airspace-scale autonomy — and the group is built in that image.
