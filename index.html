Who we are

Where control theory meets machine intelligence — and flies

AIGNC-RG is a research group of the ITU Aerospace Research Center, working at the intersection of optimization-based control, reinforcement learning, and probability theory for high-performance aerospace systems.

Our research spans highly agile unmanned aerial vehicles, GPS-denied navigation, flight trajectory optimization, airborne conflict detection and resolution, and high-level autonomy in air traffic management — carried from theory through simulation to flight test at our Örencik UAV test site. We collaborate closely with European ATM research programs and Türkiye's leading aerospace industry.

The group is led by Prof. Dr. Emre Koyuncu, Professor of Aeronautical Engineering, Director of the ITU Aerospace Research Center, and CTO of CORVUS Advanced Technologies — formerly a visiting researcher at Boeing Research & Technology Europe and MIT AeroAstro.
