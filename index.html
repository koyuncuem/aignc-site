Who we are

Where control theory meets machine intelligence — and flies

AIGNC-RG is a research group of the ITU Aerospace Research Center, working at the intersection of optimization-based control, reinforcement learning, and probability theory for high-performance aerospace systems.

Our research spans highly agile unmanned aerial vehicles, GPS-denied navigation, flight trajectory optimization, airborne conflict detection and resolution, and high-level autonomy in air traffic management — carried from theory through simulation to flight test. We collaborate closely with European ATM research programs and Türkiye's leading aerospace industry.

The group runs the AIGNC Lab and the Cyber Airspace Lab at ITU's Ayazağa campus, and contributes faculty and researchers to national and international programs on U-space, advanced air mobility, and autonomous flight.
