Who we are

Where control theory meets machine intelligence — and flies

AIGNC-RG is a research group of the ITU Aerospace Research Center, working at the intersection of optimization-based control, reinforcement learning, and probability theory for high-performance aerospace systems.

Our research spans highly agile unmanned aerial vehicles, GPS-denied navigation, flight trajectory optimization, airborne conflict detection and resolution, and high-level autonomy in air traffic management — carried from theory through simulation to flight test at our Örencik UAV test site. We collaborate closely with European ATM research programs and Türkiye's leading aerospace industry.

The group is led by Prof. Dr. Emre Koyuncu, Professor of Aeronautical Engineering, Director of the ITU Aerospace Research Center, and CTO of CORVUS Advanced Technologies — formerly a visiting researcher at Boeing Research & Technology Europe and MIT AeroAstro.

Biography

Prof. Dr. Emre Koyuncu

Google Scholar ORCID LinkedIn ITU profile

Emre Koyuncu is a Professor at Istanbul Technical University's Department of Aeronautical Engineering, the Director of the ITU Aerospace Research Center (ITU ARC), and the head of the AI-based Guidance, Navigation and Control Research Group (AIGNC-RG). He also serves as CTO of CORVUS Advanced Technologies, carrying the group's research across the lab boundary into fielded autonomous systems.

He received his B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering (2005) and M.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering (2008) from ITU, and his Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering from ITU in 2015 as a SESAR JU HALA! Research Network fellow. He was a visiting researcher at Boeing Research & Technology Europe in Madrid during 2013–2014, and at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology's Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics during 2014–2015.

His research interests lie in the broad areas of aeronautics, robotics, navigation, and control theory. In particular, he focuses on the implementation of optimization-based control, reinforcement learning, and probability theory for the design and analysis of high-performance cyber-physical systems. The application areas of his research include agile unmanned aerial vehicles; flight trajectory optimization and planning; airborne conflict detection and resolution; GPS-denied navigation; flight management and decision support systems; high-level autonomy in air traffic control systems; and modeling and resiliency assurance in large-scale air traffic networks.

Prof. Koyuncu has led numerous research grants and industrial projects funded by SESAR JU, Boeing, ASELSAN, HAVELSAN, Turkish Aerospace, TÜBİTAK, STM, Turkish Airlines, and iGA. He is an AIAA Associate Fellow (Class of 2026) and an IEEE Senior Member, serves as an Associate Editor of IEEE Access, and contributes to the IEEE CSS Technical Committee on Hybrid Systems and the AIAA Air Transportation Systems Technical Committee. His recognitions include the Boeing Early Career Grant (2015) and ITU's University–Industry Collaboration Award (2025).
